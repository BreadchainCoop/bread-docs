## About Gardens

Gardens specialises in conviction voting, a governance mechanism that enables members to show support for initiatives over time. The longer you back a proposal, the stronger your voting power becomes.

[Member Spotlight](https://paragraph.com/@breadcoop/member-spotlight-gardens)

## Bread Cooperative Integration

The Bread Cooperative Community Initiatives Pool on Gardens supports projects that advance post-capitalist values and solidarity economics. BREAD token holders can participate in conviction-based voting to fund community-led initiatives.
