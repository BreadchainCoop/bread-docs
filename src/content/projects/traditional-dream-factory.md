## About Traditional Dream Factory

Traditional Dream Factory (TDF) is a regenerative project in the Alentejo region of Portugal — one part nature conservation and rewilding, one part regenerative agriculture and ecotourism, and one part rethinking our relationship with land and ownership. Founded by Samuel Delesque nearly six years ago, what began as an abandoned chicken farm is now 25 hectares of shared land regenerated by a community of over 280 co-owners.

TDF is the first of our member organisations to have its own token, $TDF. Like BREAD, it's a governance token — holding it gives you a vote in how TDF is run. It also grants a stake in a real, physical place, with each $TDF worth a night per year at the TDF premises in Portugal.
