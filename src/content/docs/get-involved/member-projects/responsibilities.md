Once accepted, member projects are part of how the cooperative governs itself. The commitments below are what keep the network functioning.

---

## Governance participation

All member projects must have at least one representative attending monthly governance calls, providing updates, and contributing to the forward movement of the cooperative. Representatives should not miss more than one consecutive call without having a replacement present.

The nominated representative:

- is part of the [Shared Treasury](/cooperative/governance/finances/) multisig and signs transactions
- joins the monthly meetings
- gives updates on their project
- relays information from the meeting back to the rest of their team

---

## Ongoing expectations

- **Hold a minimum of 3,000 $BREAD** in your treasury
- **Add a link and logo** for Bread Cooperative to your website
- **Share with your community** that you have joined Bread Cooperative
- **Actively recruit your community** to bake $BREAD
- **Prioritise the use of $BREAD** within your community, however that looks in your context
- **Support other member projects** to increase network effects

---

## Exit and removal

A member project may leave at any time with written resignation. After resignation, Bread Cooperative and the project have 30 days to complete the necessary offboarding steps.

Member projects may also be removed by democratic process for:

- not meeting membership requirements
- becoming inactive for three or more months
- conducting activities that conflict with the [Guiding Principles](/cooperative/governance/guiding-principles/)

**The removal process:**

1. The initiator speaks to at least two others in the group to gauge support for a resolution session
2. If two others support scheduling a session, one is arranged
3. The first session focuses on understanding the issue and identifying actions the project can take to address it
4. A month is allowed for those changes to be made
5. A second session evaluates whether appropriate changes have been made, followed by a majority vote on removal

---

Core team members have a separate set of responsibilities and a separate removal process. See [Core Team](/cooperative/governance/core-team/).
