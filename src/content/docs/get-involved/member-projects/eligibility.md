Member projects are the organisations that make up the Bread Cooperative network. Each one holds a seat in the [Shared Treasury](/cooperative/governance/finances/) and receives a share of the monthly yield distribution.

To become a member project, an organisation must be active, have familiarity with web3 tools, and have demonstrated work on one or more of Bread Cooperative's [Guiding Principles](/cooperative/governance/guiding-principles/). A potential new project must be nominated by an existing member, fill out the application form, provide information about themselves, and have a representative available for monthly governance calls. New projects are only added upon unanimous vote from the whole group.

We expect projects to be comfortable being associated with the label **post-capitalist** — that we are critical of how the current system works, and want to prefigure the infrastructure for what a new one can look like. One that is more democratic and equitable for everyone, not just the few at the top.

While we use crypto tooling to facilitate the system, we don't expect every member project to be deeply involved in the crypto space. We are interested in projects that are, and in ones focused on building grassroots movements whether crypto is involved or not.

## What a good fit looks like

These lists are meant to help potential members work out how — and whether — they'd fit. A project does not need to match every point. The more they align with, the more likely they are to be accepted. If there are particularities about your organisation, explain them in the application form.

:::note[How should a potential project be organized?]

- Received no venture capital funding
- Incorporated as a non-profit or cooperative
- Governed as a cooperative / democratically
- Has an existing or planned revenue stream or model
- Organisation has at least 3 members
- Has a multi-signature wallet where crypto funds are stored
:::

:::note[What should an organizer of a potential member project have?]

- Used cryptocurrency and DeFi apps before, on Ethereum and Gnosis Chain
- Experience working with progressive grassroots groups
- Read through the [Bread Cooperative blog](https://paragraph.com/@breadcoop)
- Joined the [Bread Cooperative Guild](https://guild.xyz/breadcoop)
- Joined the [Bread Cooperative Discord](https://discord.com/invite/zmNqsHRHDa)
- [Baked at least 10 $BREAD](/cooperative/currency/how-to-bake/), preferably much more
- Read our [Guiding Principles](/cooperative/governance/guiding-principles/)
- Applied through the project form — see [How to Apply](/get-involved/member-projects/how-to-apply/)
:::

:::note[What kinds of projects would we like to see join?]

This is a collection of use cases shared by current members of Bread Cooperative. Don't take it as the only kinds of initiatives we will accept.

- An aligned physical community space
- Existing labor unions and cooperatives interested in or already exploring the use of crypto
- Local community organisations interested in exploring the use of crypto
- Crypto-based savings circles like a Susu
- Artist and developer collectives
- Tools that make it easier to organise labor unions and strike action
- Decentralised academic / research institutes
- Decentralised co-op / commune platforms
- Decentralised non-profit org platforms
:::
