There are two ways in, and they work differently. Pick the one that matches who's asking.

---

## Contributors

For individuals. You bring a skill — development, writing, design, marketing, community building, business development — and put it to work on the cooperative itself. Contributions can be continuous or one-off, paid or volunteer.

There's no gate to clear before you start. Join the Discord, come to a community call, take on a task. Contributors who stick around can be nominated to the [core team](/cooperative/governance/core-team/).

## Member Projects

For organisations. Member projects make up the Bread Cooperative network: each holds a seat in the [Shared Treasury](/cooperative/governance/finances/) multisig and receives a share of the monthly yield distribution from the [Solidarity Fund](/tools/solidarity-fund/how-it-works/).

This one does have a gate. Projects are nominated by an existing member and accepted only by unanimous vote, and membership comes with ongoing commitments — monthly governance calls, a minimum $BREAD holding, and active use of $BREAD in your community.

- [Responsibilities](/get-involved/member-projects/responsibilities/) — what's expected once you're in
- [How to Apply](/get-involved/member-projects/how-to-apply/) — the application process
