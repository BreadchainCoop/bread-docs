## BREAD Solidarity Fund

Give without giving. Bake BREAD to generate yield, distributed monthly to member projects by community vote. Your principal stays intact — only the earnings get shared.

[Learn more](/tools/solidarity-fund/)

## Stacks

Savings circles for those closest to you. Members pool BREAD at regular intervals and take turns receiving the full pot — the centuries-old rotating savings model, now running on tamper-proof escrow with no bank, no lender, and no single person holding the funds.

[Learn more](/tools/stacks/)
