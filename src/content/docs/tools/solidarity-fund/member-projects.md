Every month, yield generated by the Bread Solidarity Fund is distributed to these projects by community vote. Each one is part of the Bread Cooperative network and holds a seat in the Shared Treasury.

A share of each monthly distribution also goes to the **Bread Cooperative Shared Treasury**. Rather than being paid out, this share is returned to the fund, where it adds to the pool that generates yield — compounding what's available to distribute in future. It's counted among the parties that receive a distribution, but isn't a member organisation in its own right.
