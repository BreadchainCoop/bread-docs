---
title: Connecting a Wallet
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

1. Go to the [Solidarity Fund app](https://fund.bread.coop/).
2. Click the connect button in the top-right corner of the page.
3. Select your wallet from the list. If you're using a mobile wallet, choose WalletConnect and scan the QR code with your device.
4. Approve the connection in your wallet when prompted.

We recommend using a desktop or laptop. More screen space makes the process easier.

If your wallet isn't appearing in the list, check that the browser extension is installed, up to date, and that you're signed in with the right account.
